Bio

Juan Eduardo Flores is a multidisciplinary artist and educator born in Del Rio, Texas, and raised on the southeast side of San Antonio. He received his BA in Art from Connecticut College and his MFA in Art and Technology Studies from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago in 2023. As the recipient of the Mortimer Hays-Brandeis Fellowship, he spent over a year in Mexico City concentrating on sound art. His start was in the Blue Star Contemporary Art Museum's MOSAIC program, making murals out of handmade mosaic tiles with other high school students. He teaches new media art at the University of Illinois Chicago.

Artist statement

Juan Flores is an artist working with sound, motion, and code. He is interested in what a message keeps, and what it lets go, on its journey from one place to another. Drawing on information theory, he listens for the noise in every passage and asks how little is needed to carry something across.
