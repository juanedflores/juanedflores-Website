Bio

Juan Eduardo Flores was born in Del Rio, Texas, and raised on the southeast side of San Antonio. He received his BA in Art from Connecticut College as a fellow of the Ammerman Center for Arts & Technology, and his MFA in Art and Technology Studies from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago in 2023. As the recipient of the Mortimer Hays-Brandeis Fellowship, he spent over a year in Mexico City committing to a sound art practice. His start was in the Blue Star Contemporary Art Museum's MOSAIC program, making murals out of handmade mosaic tiles with other high school students.

He currently teaches in the School of Art & Art History at the University of Illinois Chicago.

Artist statement

I am interested in what a message keeps, and what it lets go, on its journey from one place to another. Drawing on information theory, I listen for the noise in every passage and ask how little is needed to carry something across.
